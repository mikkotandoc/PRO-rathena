## First Login Checklist

After creating your first character in Prontera:

- [ ] Talk to **Healer** (`prontera 162,193`) for buffs
- [ ] Visit **Job Master** (`prontera 153,193`) when ready to change jobs
- [ ] Open **Warper** (`prontera 159,192`) to explore leveling areas
- [ ] Check **Episode Guide** (`prontera 165,175`) if pursuing storyline content
- [ ] Review **Centro** (`prontera 166,300`) for Booster Coin items
- [ ] Join **Eden Group** via Eden HQ (`moc_para01`) for beginner missions

## Job Progression

The **Job Master** supports:

- 1st → 2nd → Transcendent → 3rd → **4th class**
- Expanded classes (Taekwon, Gunslinger, Ninja, Summoner, etc.)
- Platinum skills via **Platinum Skill NPC** (`prontera 128,200`)
