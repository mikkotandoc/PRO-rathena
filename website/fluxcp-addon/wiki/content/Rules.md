# Server Rules

[← Back to Home](Home.md)

Default rules for **PRO-Ragnarok**. *(Staff: edit this page to match your official policy.)*

---

## General Conduct

| # | Rule |
|---|------|
| 1 | Treat all players and staff with respect — no harassment, hate speech, or discrimination. |
| 2 | English is the primary language in public channels (`#global`, `#trade`, `#map`). |
| 3 | No spamming, advertising other servers, or disruptive behavior in chat or vending. |
| 4 | Impersonating staff or other players is forbidden. |

---

## Account & Security

| # | Rule |
|---|------|
| 1 | **One person per account** — account sharing is at your own risk; staff may not recover stolen items from shared accounts. |
| 2 | Keep your password and **pincode** private. Staff will **never** ask for your password. |
| 3 | Report compromised accounts to staff immediately via `#support` or Discord. |
| 4 | Trading accounts for real money or other games is **forbidden** (see RMT below). |

---

## Gameplay & Fair Play

| # | Rule |
|---|------|
| 1 | **No bots, macros, or third-party automation** that plays the game for you. The server runs **macro patrol** on PRO-Asia coin farming maps. |
| 2 | No packet editing, speed hacks, noclip, damage hacks, or modified clients that give unfair advantage. |
| 3 | Exploiting bugs for personal gain is prohibited — report bugs to staff; abusers may be banned. |
| 4 | Multi-clienting *(staff: allow or disallow — edit this line)*. |

---

## Trading & Economy

| # | Rule |
|---|------|
| 1 | **Real Money Trading (RMT)** — buying or selling zeny, items, or accounts for real-world money is **not allowed** unless staff say otherwise. |
| 2 | Trade scams are your responsibility — use trade window carefully and record valuable trades. |
| 3 | Staff do not mediate every trade dispute; clear evidence (screenshots/video) may be required. |
| 4 | PRO-Asia Coins and playtime points have **no real-world cash value**. |

---

## PvP, WoE & Events

| # | Rule |
|---|------|
| 1 | **War of Emperium** — Tue/Thu 21:00–23:00, Sat 16:00–18:00. Follow alliance agreements you make; betrayal is gameplay, not a rule violation. |
| 2 | No intentional lagging, emperium bug abuse, or blocking castles with unintended mechanics. |
| 3 | **PVP Ladder** — register fairly; leaving during bracket may forfeit rewards. |
| 4 | Spawn camping in towns is discouraged; repeated griefing may be punished. |

---

## Staff & Support

| # | Rule |
|---|------|
| 1 | Staff decisions are final. Argue respectfully in tickets/Discord, not public flame wars. |
| 2 | Do not impersonate GMs or falsely claim staff authority. |
| 3 | Use `#support` or Discord for help — not GM harassment whispers. |

---

## Penalties (Default)

| Offense | Typical action |
|---------|----------------|
| Minor chat toxicity | Warning → mute |
| Harassment / hate speech | Mute → temp ban |
| Botting / macros | **Permanent ban** |
| Hacking / RMT | **Permanent ban** |
| Exploit abuse | Item removal + ban |
